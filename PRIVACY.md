# Privacy Policy — 15-minute-daily-bot

**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

This policy describes what data 15-minute-daily-bot ("the Bot") collects, why, and how you can control it.

## 1. Data We Collect

**Emoji votes (contest forum):** The Bot reads message content only in the designated contest forum, to count emoji reactions used as votes, and to verify users are submitting supported image attachments. Message contents and/or attachments are not stored.

**Theme submissions:** When you use the submission slash command, the Bot opens a modal with two form fields. On submit, we store:
- The submitted theme title and optional description
- Your Discord username and id
- The server (guild) name and id the submission came from

No other message content, DMs, or attachments are collected or stored anywhere.

- Theme submissions: 

We do not sell, share, or use this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond running the contest.

## 3. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored in a database on a private server hosted via DigitalOcean. Vote tallies and message content are processed by the Bot and not saved or sent to any third party. Discord itself processes message and interaction data as part of normal bot operation, per [Discord's own Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

Theme submissions are stored until you remove them yourself. You can delete your saved theme submission at any time using the theme command (/daily-theme), and pressing the "Clear Theme" button, which permanently deletes it from the database.

For anything you can't remove yourself, contact us (see below) and we'll delete it.

## 5. Your Rights

You can:
- View what theme you currently have stored (via /daily-theme)
- Delete your own submission at any time (via dialy-theme)
- Request deletion of any other data we hold about you by contacting us

## 6. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, open an issue on our GitHub: https://github.com/ricdoric/daily-drawing-bot/issues

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the Bot changes. Material changes will be noted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
